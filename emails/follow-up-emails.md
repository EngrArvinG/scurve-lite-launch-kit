## Sending tips

- For a handful of people, send each email yourself from Gmail using BCC (never put everyone in the To/CC fields).
- For more than a few dozen, use a mail-merge tool that works with Google Sheets, so each person gets their own copy and you can honour "remove" requests.
- Delete a person's row from the Responses sheet (and your mailing list) when they ask to be removed.
