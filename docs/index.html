Questions

Is the Lite file really free?

Yes. No payment and no credit card. It is limited to 10 activities and 26 weeks.

Which Excel versions work?

Microsoft Excel 2013 or newer on Windows or Mac, including Microsoft 365. Phones and tablets can open it, but entering data is easier on a computer.

Does it use macros?

No. It is plain Excel formulas with no macros, add-ins or internet connection.

Can I use it in Google Sheets?

No. It is built for Microsoft Excel. Charts and formatting may not work in other programs.

Can I use pesos or another currency?

The workbook is built and labelled in US dollars. You can type amounts in another currency and change the cell format, but the headings will still say USD.

Does it replace MS Project or Primavera?

No. It has no task dependencies or critical path. It is a simple progress and cost report: Gantt, S-curve, SPI, CPI and forecast finish.

The file opens in Protected View. What do I do?

Click “Enable Editing” at the top of Excel so the formulas can calculate.
